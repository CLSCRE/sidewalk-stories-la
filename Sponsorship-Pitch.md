## The Opportunity

Your business serves this neighborhood every day. Now you can put your name on a permanent piece of public art that makes the streets safer and more beautiful.

Sidewalk Stories LA repairs broken sidewalk panels and transforms them into community mosaics designed and built by neighborhood kids. We are launching our pilot in [neighborhood name] this year, and we are looking for 5 local business sponsors.

## What $750 Buys

- **One complete mosaic panel** in your neighborhood: concrete repair, mosaic materials, teaching artist stipend, and city permit
- **Your logo or name on the mosaic panel** itself: permanent public recognition
- **Professional before and after photography** of the panel, delivered to you for marketing use
- **Social media campaign** tagging your business and the young artists who built it
- **Press release mention** naming your business as a founding sponsor
- **Plaque or inscription** on the panel crediting your sponsorship

## Why This Works for Local Businesses

**Visibility:** Your logo sits on a public art piece that every pedestrian, neighbor, and visitor sees. It is not a billboard. It is a contribution to the neighborhood.

**Story:** You get professional photography and social content showing your business invested in the community. That is content worth sharing.

**Connection:** The kids who build the mosaic live here. Their parents live here. Their grandparents walk past your panel. That is real local relationship building.

**Cost:** $750 is modest. It is less than most small business advertising budgets and produces something permanent.

## The Panel Process

1. You sponsor a panel. We assign it a place in the queue.
2. The community votes on which damaged panel gets repaired next.
3. A licensed concrete crew fixes the panel.
4. Neighborhood kids and a teaching artist design and build the mosaic.
5. We document everything and celebrate the finished panel.
6. Your logo goes on the panel. Your business gets tagged in all content.
