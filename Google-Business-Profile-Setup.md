# Sidewalk Stories LA — Google Business Profile Setup Guide

_Pre-filled content and step-by-step instructions to create and optimize the Google Business Profile in one session._

---

## Why This Matters

A Google Business Profile ensures that when people search "sidewalk repair nonprofit Los Angeles" or "youth public art LA," Sidewalk Stories LA appears in Google Maps and local search results. It is free and takes about 15 minutes to set up.

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Go to** business.google.com/add
2. **Sign in** with your Google Workspace account (trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org)
3. **Enter business name:** Sidewalk Stories LA
4. **Select category:** Nonprofit Organization
5. **Add location:** Yes (we serve the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood)
   - Address: 7951 Blackburn Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90048
6. **Service areas:** Select "I deliver goods and services to my customers" and add:
   - Fairfax, Los Angeles, CA
   - Mid-City West, Los Angeles, CA
   - West Hollywood, CA
   - Los Angeles, CA (broader area)
7. **Contact details:**
   - Phone: [Your phone number]
   - Website: sidewalkstoriesla.org
8. **Verify:** Google will send a postcard to the address with a verification code. This takes 5-14 days.

## Business Description (750 characters max)

**Copy and paste this:**

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit that repairs broken sidewalk panels and transforms them into community-built mosaic art designed and installed by neighborhood youth working alongside professional teaching artists. Our Year 1 pilot will complete 10 panels in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood. We accept donations, corporate sponsorships, and volunteer support. Founded in 2026 by Trevor Damyan.
