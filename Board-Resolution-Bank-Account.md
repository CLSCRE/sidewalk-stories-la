# Board Resolution — Authorization to Open Bank Account

_Resolution of the Board of Directors of Sidewalk Stories LA_

---

**WHEREAS,** Sidewalk Stories LA (the "Corporation") was duly incorporated as a California nonprofit public benefit corporation on [Date]; and

**WHEREAS,** the Corporation requires a bank account to receive donations, grants, and sponsorships and to pay expenses; and

**WHEREAS,** the Board has determined that [Bank Name] is an appropriate depository for the Corporation's funds;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED** that the Board of Directors hereby authorizes the opening of a checking account in the name of Sidewalk Stories LA at [Bank Name], located at [Branch Address].

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED** that Trevor Damyan, Board President, and [Treasurer Name], Treasurer, are hereby authorized as signatories on said account, with either signature sufficient for withdrawals up to $[amount]; signatures of both required for withdrawals exceeding $[amount].

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED** that the Corporation's Employer Identification Number (EIN) is [EIN].

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED** that the Secretary is authorized to certify copies of this resolution and deliver them to [Bank Name] as evidence of this authorization.

---

**ADOPTED** by the Board of Directors on [Date], by unanimous vote of the directors present.
