## Email 1 — Community Partners

**To:** info@communitypartners.org
**From:** trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org
**Subject:** Fiscal sponsorship inquiry: youth sidewalk repair and public art program

Hi Community Partners team,

I am launching a new Los Angeles project called Sidewalk Stories LA and I am looking for a fiscal sponsor for the pilot year.

The program repairs cracked and hazardous sidewalk panels and transforms each repair into a small public mosaic designed and built by neighborhood kids with a teaching artist and a licensed concrete crew. Residents submit damaged sidewalk photos through a mobile app, the community votes, and the top panel each month gets fixed and turned into art. It combines public safety, youth arts, and civic engagement in one visible, repeatable neighborhood project.

For the pilot we plan to complete 10 panels in the Fairfax / Mid-City West area with a Year 1 budget of approximately $38,700. We have a one page prospectus, a financial model, and a founding board of three directors ready to go. I have worked in community finance and real estate in Los Angeles for several years and I am building this with a technology partner who already built the sidewalk reporting app we plan to use.

I would like to learn whether Community Partners currently has capacity to sponsor a project like this and what your fee structure, insurance coverage, and onboarding timeline look like. If it makes sense, I can send our prospectus and set up a brief call.

Thank you for the work you do for LA projects like this one.

Best,
Trevor Damyan
Founder, Sidewalk Stories LA

trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org

Hi Arts for LA team,

I am reaching out because Sidewalk Stories LA sits right at the intersection of public art, youth education, and neighborhood improvement, and I want to see whether Arts for LA offers fiscal sponsorship for a project like ours.

The idea is simple: we repair broken sidewalk panels in Los Angeles neighborhoods and convert each repair into a community built mosaic, designed by local kids alongside a professional teaching artist and installed by a licensed concrete crew. Residents report damaged panels through a mobile app, the community votes, and we complete one panel at a time so the result is immediate, visible, and locally owned.

Our Year 1 pilot targets 10 panels in the Fairfax / Mid-City West area with a budget of about $38,700. We have a board of three founding directors, a one page funder prospectus, a detailed financial model, and a technology partner whose sidewalk reporting app will handle submissions and voting.

Could you tell me whether Arts for LA sponsors startup arts projects, what your administrative fee and services include, and whether you provide insurance coverage for youth program activities on public right of way? I am happy to send our materials and schedule a call if there is a fit.

Thank you. I appreciate any guidance you can offer.
