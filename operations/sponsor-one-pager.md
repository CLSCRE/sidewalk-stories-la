### Year 1 Pilot: Fairfax / Mid-City West

Sidewalk Stories LA repairs broken sidewalk panels and transforms them into community-built mosaics. Each panel is designed by neighborhood youth ages 10 to 17, guided by a professional teaching artist, and installed by a licensed concrete crew.

**The problem:** Cracked, lifted sidewalk panels cause trips, falls, and wheelchair access issues across Los Angeles. City repair backlogs are long, and many panels stay broken for years.

**Our solution:** Turn each hazard into a neighborhood story. Safer sidewalks, youth arts education, and public art everyone can be proud of.

## Back Side

### How It Works

1. **Report:** Residents photograph cracked panels.
2. **Design:** Youth create mosaic designs with a teaching artist.
3. **Approve:** Property owners and city permits are secured.
4. **Repair:** Licensed crews repair the concrete.
5. **Install:** Community installs the mosaic.
6. **Celebrate:** The neighborhood unveils its new public art.

---

### Year 1 Budget at a Glance
