## Campaign Tagline / Short Description
Turn neighborhood tripping hazards into public art designed by local youth and professional teaching artists.

## Story

Hi, I am Trevor Damyan, founder of Sidewalk Stories LA.

Every year, broken sidewalk panels cause trips, falls, and wheelchair access problems across Los Angeles. City repair backlogs are long, and many cracks stay cracked for years. Meanwhile, neighborhood kids need positive, hands-on public art programs close to home.

Sidewalk Stories LA fixes both problems at once.

We repair broken sidewalk panels and transform them into community-built mosaics. Each panel is designed by neighborhood youth ages 10 to 17, guided by a professional teaching artist, and installed by a licensed concrete crew. The result is safer sidewalks, real youth arts education, and public art the whole neighborhood can be proud of.

Our Year 1 pilot is a 10 panel project in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood. Every dollar raised goes directly to concrete, tile, teaching artist stipends, permits, and safety equipment.

How your donation helps:
- $25 buys tile for one mosaic panel
- $100 covers part of a teaching artist stipend
- $400 funds one city permit
- $750 sponsors a full panel and puts your name on it
- $2,700 funds an entire panel from cracked concrete to finished mosaic

The first panel is planned for Fall 2026. With your support, we will turn a tripping hazard into a neighborhood story.

Thank you for helping us repair sidewalks, build community, and prove that public art can start at your own front steps.
