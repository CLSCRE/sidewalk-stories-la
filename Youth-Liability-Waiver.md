## ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Sidewalk Stories LA provides hands-on public art experiences for youth participants. Activities may include, but are not limited to:
- Sketching and designing mosaic patterns
- Handling tile, grout, and sealant materials
- Working outdoors on public sidewalks
- Using hand tools under adult supervision
- Participating in community events and photography

All activities are supervised by a professional teaching artist and Sidewalk Stories LA volunteers.

## ASSUMPTION OF RISK

I understand and acknowledge that participation in Sidewalk Stories LA activities involves inherent risks, including but not limited to:
