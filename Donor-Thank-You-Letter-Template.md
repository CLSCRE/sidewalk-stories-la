# Sidewalk Stories LA — Donor Thank-You Letter Template

_Use this template for individual donations of any amount. Customize the opening based on the donor's relationship to Trevor._

---

## Version 1: Personal Friend / Family

---

Dear [First Name],

Thank you so much for your generous donation of $[Amount] to Sidewalk Stories LA. Your support means more than I can put into words.

Because of you, we are one step closer to repairing our first broken sidewalk panel and turning it into a small public mosaic built by neighborhood kids. That cracked piece of concrete near [Street Name] is going to become something beautiful, and you helped make it happen.

Your gift goes directly to materials: tile, grout, sealant, and safety gear for the kids who will design and install the mosaic. Every dollar counts in a startup nonprofit, and yours just moved us closer to our first panel.

I will make sure you see the before and after photos. When that mosaic is finished, I will send you a picture of the kids standing next to it. That is the moment your donation becomes real.

With gratitude,

Trevor Damyan

## Delivery Tips

1. **Send within 48 hours** of receiving the donation.
2. **Personalize the opening.** Reference how you know the donor.
3. **Include a specific use.** Donors love knowing exactly what their money bought.
4. **Attach a photo** if you have one (even a stock photo of mosaic tiles works early on).
5. **Follow up with a photo** of the completed panel when it happens.
6. **Keep a spreadsheet** of donor names, amounts, dates, and which version of the letter was sent.
