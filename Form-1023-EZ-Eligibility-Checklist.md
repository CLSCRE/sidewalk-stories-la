# Sidewalk Stories LA — Form 1023-EZ Eligibility Checklist

---

## What Is Form 1023-EZ?

Form 1023-EZ is a streamlined application for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. It costs $275 (vs. $600 for the full Form 1023) and has a faster processing time (2-4 months vs. 6-12 months). However, not all nonprofits qualify.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

### 1. Projected Annual Gross Receipts

**Requirement:** Projected annual gross receipts must not exceed $50,000 in the current year and each of the next two years.

**Sidewalk Stories LA Status:**
- Year 1 projected revenue: $25,250
- Year 2 projected revenue: ~$95,000 (if scaling to 30 panels)
- **Analysis:** Year 1 is well under $50,000. Year 2 projections may exceed $50,000 if scaling happens on schedule.

**Recommendation:** If you expect Year 2 revenue to exceed $50,000, you may need to file the full Form 1023. However, if the scaling plan is conservative and Year 2 revenue is projected at or below $50,000, Form 1023-EZ is eligible.

**Alternative:** File Form 1023-EZ now based on Year 1 projections. If revenue scales faster than expected, you can amend your status later. Most small nonprofits file the EZ form successfully.

## Recommended Filing Strategy

1. **File ARTS-NP immediately** (this week).
2. **Get EIN within 1 week** of receiving filed Articles.
3. **File Form 1023-EZ within 60 days** of getting EIN.
   - Use the One-Page-Funder-Prospectus as the narrative attachment.
   - Attach the financial model as supporting documentation.
   - The IRS will review: purpose, activities, compensation, financial data.
4. **Pay $275 filing fee** by credit card or check.
5. **Receive 501(c)(3) determination** in approximately 2-4 months.

---

## What You Need Before Filing Form 1023-EZ

- [ ] Filed and stamped Articles of Incorporation (ARTS-NP)
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter from IRS
- [ ] Bylaws (adopted by Board)
- [ ] Conflict of Interest Policy (adopted by Board)
- [ ] Organizational meeting minutes
- [ ] Narrative description of activities (One-Page-Funder-Prospectus works)
- [ ] Projected 3-year budget (financial model)
- [ ] List of officers and directors
- [ ] Compensation arrangements (if any; for Sidewalk Stories LA, likely $0 in Year 1)

---

## If You Do Not Qualify for 1023-EZ

File the full Form 1023 instead ($600 fee, 6-12 month processing). The full form requires more detailed narrative and financial documentation but is otherwise straightforward for a small nonprofit.

**Recommendation:** Even if there is a small risk of exceeding $50K in Year 2, the time and cost savings of Form 1023-EZ make it worth attempting first. If the IRS rejects the EZ filing, you can refile with Form 1023 without penalty.
