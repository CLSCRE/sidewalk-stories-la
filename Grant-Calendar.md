## Submission Checklist by Funder

### Awesome Foundation LA
- [ ] One-paragraph project description (online form)
- [ ] Optional: link to website or video
- [ ] No budget required
- **Next step:** Submit online at awesomefoundation.org/en/chapters/la

### Mid-City West Neighborhood Council
- [ ] Attend one Neighborhood Council board meeting
- [ ] Submit CD 11 Community Improvement Grant application
- [ ] Include: map of project location, brief budget, community benefit statement
- **Next step:** Check outreach@midcitywest.org for next meeting date and grant cycle

### California Community Foundation (CCF)
- [ ] Confirm 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor
- [ ] Draft 2-page narrative (mission, need, program, outcomes)
- [ ] Prepare project budget and organizational budget
- [ ] Attach board list, IRS determination letter (or fiscal sponsor agreement)
- **Next step:** Review CCF responsive grant guidelines at calfund.org

### LA County Arts Commission
- [ ] Confirm nonprofit status or fiscal sponsor
- [ ] Draft project description with strong visual component
- [ ] Include teaching artist resume and youth outreach plan
- [ ] Prepare documentation plan (photos, video, social media)
- **Next step:** Check lacountyarts.org for 2026–2027 grant calendar

### City of LA DCA
- [ ] Confirm nonprofit status or fiscal sponsor
- [ ] Draft project narrative emphasizing neighborhood access and youth participation
- [ ] Include community support letters (neighborhood council, property owners)
- **Next step:** Check culturela.org for Cultural Development and Public Art grant cycles

### Weingart Foundation
- [ ] Draft 1-page letter of inquiry (LOI)
- [ ] Include: mission, target population, expected outcomes, budget summary
- [ ] Attach 501(c)(3) determination letter or fiscal sponsor agreement
- **Next step:** Submit LOI via weingartfdn.org online portal
