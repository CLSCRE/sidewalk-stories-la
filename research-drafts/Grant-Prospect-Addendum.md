# Sidewalk Stories LA: Grant Prospect Addendum

_At least 15 additional grant opportunities not already listed in Grant-Prospect-Shortlist.md. Focused on Los Angeles County / City funders, California arts and youth development funders, and national community arts foundations. Research current as of 2026-07-20._

---

## Local / LA County Funders

### 1. Los Angeles County Department of Arts and Culture - Community Impact Arts Grant (CIAG)
- **Website:** [lacountyarts.org](https://lacountyarts.org)
- **Program:** Community Impact Arts Grant (CIAG)
- **Typical award:** $5,000 to $20,000
- **Deadline:** Annual; 2026-27 cycle closed September 30, 2025. Monitor for 2027-28.
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofit organizations whose primary mission is outside the arts, municipal departments, tribal governments, and fiscally sponsored projects in LA County. 1:1 cash match required.
- **Fit rationale:** Sidewalk Stories LA is fundamentally a pedestrian safety / youth civic engagement project that uses mosaic art as its method, making CIAG a strong fit because it funds non-arts organizations using arts and culture.

### 2. Los Angeles County Department of Arts and Culture - Organizational Grant Program (OGP)
- **Website:** [lacountyarts.org](https://lacountyarts.org)
- **Program:** Organizational Grant Program (OGP)
- **Typical award:** Calculated by organizational budget size via online grant calculator
- **Deadline:** Annual; 2026-27 cycle closed September 30, 2025. Monitor for future cycle.
- **Eligibility:** LA County arts nonprofits with more than 50% of budget devoted to arts programming; fiscally sponsored projects with Model A agreements. Dollar-for-dollar cash match required.
- **Fit rationale:** If Sidewalk Stories LA is framed primarily as an arts organization, OGP could support general operating costs and teaching artist programming.

### 3. California Arts Council - Arts and Youth Grant
- **Website:** [grants.ca.gov/grants/arts-and-youth/](https://www.grants.ca.gov/grants/arts-and-youth/)
- **Program:** Arts and Youth
- **Typical award:** Up to $25,000
- **Deadline:** May 12, 2026, 11:59 PM
- **Eligibility:** California-based 501(c)(3) nonprofits, municipal/county/tribal government units, and fiscally sponsored organizations. Serves youth ages 0-25.
- **Fit rationale:** Directly funds arts participation and learning for young people, with priority for under-resourced communities and first-time grantees; Sidewalk Stories LA’s youth mosaic workshops align cleanly.

### 4. California Arts Council - Local Impact Grant
- **Website:** [arts.ca.gov](https://arts.ca.gov)
- **Program:** Local Impact
- **Typical award:** Up to $19,000 (recent cohort data)
- **Deadline:** Annual; monitor 2026-27 calendar
- **Eligibility:** California nonprofits and fiscally sponsored projects providing arts programs in underserved communities.
- **Fit rationale:** Piece by Piece Los Angeles received this grant for community-based mosaic workshops in Skid Row, suggesting a similar project serving Fairfax / Mid-City West could compete.

### 5. City of Los Angeles Department of Cultural Affairs - Cultural Development Grant
- **Website:** [culturela.org](https://culturela.org)
- **Program:** Cultural Development Grant
- **Typical award:** $5,000 to $20,000
- **Deadline:** Typically August/September; confirm 2026 cycle
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofit arts organizations or fiscally sponsored projects operating within City of LA boundaries.
- **Fit rationale:** DCA funds neighborhood arts access and youth programming; Mid-City West is inside City of LA boundaries.

### 6. City of Los Angeles Department of Cultural Affairs - Public Art Division / Public Works Improvements Arts Program (PWIAP)
- **Website:** [culture.lacity.gov/about/public-art-division](https://culture.lacity.gov/about/public-art-division)
- **Program:** Public Works Improvements Arts Program (PWIAP) and Percent for Art
- **Typical award:** Variable; tied to capital projects
- **Deadline:** Project-driven
- **Eligibility:** City capital improvement projects and eligible nonprofit partners.
- **Fit rationale:** If the pilot can be linked to a city sidewalk repair or beautification project, PWIAP could fund the art component.

### 7. Mid-City West Neighborhood Council - Community Improvement Grant
- **Website:** [midcitywest.org](https://www.midcitywest.org/)
- **Program:** CD 11 Community Improvement Grant (Neighborhood Purpose Grant)
- **Typical award:** $500 to $5,000
- **Deadline:** Quarterly; next cycle likely September/October 2026
- **Eligibility:** Projects benefiting the Mid-City West Community Plan area.
- **Fit rationale:** Hyper-local funding for a hyper-local pilot; attending one board meeting is required and builds relationship simultaneously.

### 8. Los Angeles Neighborhood Council Coalition / Mayor’s Office of Community Engagement
- **Website:** [ EmpowerLA.org ](http://empowerla.org)
- **Program:** Neighborhood Purpose Grants (various councils)
- **Typical award:** $500 to $5,000 per council
- **Deadline:** Varies by council
- **Eligibility:** Projects within specific Neighborhood Council boundaries.
- **Fit rationale:** Adjacent councils to Mid-City West (e.g., Greater Wilshire, P.I.C.O., West Hollywood West if expanding) each have small discretionary funds.

## Southern California Foundations

### 9. The Ahmanson Foundation
- **Website:** [ahmansonfoundation.org](https://www.ahmansonfoundation.org)
- **Program:** General operating and project grants
- **Typical award:** $10,000 to $50,000
- **Deadline:** Rolling via letter of inquiry
- **Eligibility:** Southern California nonprofits, primarily Los Angeles County.
- **Fit rationale:** Ahmanson funds arts, education, and community development; a mosaic youth public art project in LA County fits all three program areas.

### 10. The Getty Foundation - PST Art Grants (formerly Pacific Standard Time)
- **Website:** [getty.edu/foundation/initiatives/current/pst_art.html](https://www.getty.edu/foundation/initiatives/current/pst_art.html)
- **Program:** PST Art Grants
- **Typical award:** $50,000 to $200,000+ (major exhibitions)
- **Deadline:** Themed; next major cycle around 2027-2028
- **Eligibility:** Museums, universities, and arts nonprofits with significant programming capacity.
- **Fit rationale:** This is a stretch for Year 1, but if Sidewalk Stories LA partners with an established arts institution on a public art / youth exhibition, PST Art could become relevant.

### 11. The Eli and Edythe Broad Foundation
- **Website:** [broadfoundation.org](https://www.broadfoundation.org)
- **Program:** Arts and civic engagement
- **Typical award:** Variable; often large
- **Deadline:** Invitation/relationship-driven
- **Eligibility:** Los Angeles nonprofits with strong track records.
- **Fit rationale:** Long-term target for scale; not a Year 1 cold prospect, but worth tracking as the portfolio of panels grows.

### 12. The Kenneth T. and Eileen L. Norris Foundation
- **Website:** [norrisfoundation.org](https://www.norrisfoundation.org)
- **Program:** Youth, education, and arts in Southern California
- **Typical award:** $5,000 to $25,000
- **Deadline:** Annual; check guidelines
- **Eligibility:** Southern California nonprofits.
- **Fit rationale:** Youth development with hands-on learning is a core Norris priority; mosaic design and installation qualifies.

### 13. The Sheri and Les Biller Family Foundation
- **Website:** [billerfamilyfoundation.org](https://www.billerfamilyfoundation.org)
- **Program:** Arts education and youth development
- **Typical award:** $10,000 to $50,000

- **Eligibility:** LA County nonprofits with youth arts education programs.
- **Fit rationale:** Strong alignment with teaching artist-led youth programming in underserved LA neighborhoods.

## National Community Arts / Youth / Civic Funders

### 14. National Endowment for the Arts - Challenge America
- **Website:** [arts.gov](https://www.arts.gov)
- **Program:** Challenge America
- **Typical award:** $10,000
- **Deadline:** Annual, typically spring
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor required; 1:1 match required.
- **Fit rationale:** Fast-track NEA funding for underserved populations; Sidewalk Stories LA’s combination of youth, public art, and accessibility makes it competitive once 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsorship is in place.

### 15. National Endowment for the Arts - Our Town
- **Website:** [arts.gov](https://www.arts.gov)
- **Program:** Our Town (creative placemaking)
- **Typical award:** $25,000 to $150,000
- **Deadline:** Annual; typically late summer
- **Eligibility:** Partnerships between local governments and arts/cultural organizations.
- **Fit rationale:** Requires a local government partner (e.g., City of LA DCA, Council District office, or Neighborhood Council). High potential if that relationship can be formalized.

### 16. The Surdna Foundation - Thriving Cultures
- **Website:** [surdna.org](https://www.surdna.org)
- **Program:** Thriving Cultures
- **Typical award:** $25,000 to $150,000
- **Deadline:** Annual RFP, typically spring
- **Eligibility:** Arts and culture organizations engaging youth in shaping the built environment.
- **Fit rationale:** Surdna explicitly funds creative placemaking and youth-led civic art; a sidewalk mosaic program is a textbook fit, though competition is fierce.

### 17. The Robert R. McCormick Foundation
- **Website:** [mccormickfdn.org](https://www.mccormickfdn.org)
- **Program:** Communities Program / Civic Engagement
- **Typical award:** $10,000 to $50,000
- **Deadline:** Annual
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofits in Chicagoland and select national markets; less LA-focused.
- **Fit rationale:** National civic engagement funder; a stretch on geography but worth monitoring if McCormick opens broader California funding.

### 18. The William Penn Foundation
- **Website:** [williampennfoundation.org](https://www.williampennfoundation.org)
- **Program:** Creative expression / Watershed / Public space
- **Typical award:** $10,000 to $75,000
- **Deadline:** Rolling / annual
- **Eligibility:** Philadelphia region primarily; national only by invitation.
- **Fit rationale:** Geographic mismatch for Year 1, but their public-space / youth arts model is instructive for proposal language.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Apply immediately** to California Arts Council Arts and Youth (deadline May 12, 2026). This is the only confirmed open grant on this list as of July 2026.
2. **Confirm fiscal sponsor or 501(c)(3) status** before August 15, 2026, because most of these funders require it.
3. **Monitor** LA County Arts and Culture and City of LA DCA websites weekly in August and September 2026 for new cycle announcements.
4. **Submit LOI** to Ahmanson Foundation once the pilot has at least one confirmed panel and a fiscal sponsor letter.
5. **Build relationship** with Sony Pictures Entertainment community affairs team and Culver City / LA corporate social responsibility contacts.
