## Part 3: Key Coverages Sidewalk Stories LA Should Request

| Coverage | Why it matters | Estimated ask |
|---|---|---|
| **General Liability** | Bodily injury, property damage, personal/advertising injury on public sidewalks | $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate minimum |
| **Volunteer / Participant Accident** | Medical payments if a youth or volunteer is injured during a workshop | Medical payments coverage or accident policy |
| **Sexual Misconduct / Abuse Liability** | Critical for any program involving minors | Must be explicit in policy |
| **Special Event Insurance** | One-day coverage for panel unveiling ceremonies, vote events, fundraisers | Per-event or annual policy |
| **Directors & Officers (D&O)** | Protects board members from governance lawsuits | $500K to $1M |
| **Property / Inland Marine** | Covers art supplies, tile inventory, tools, equipment | Replacement cost basis |
| **Cyber Liability** | Donor and participant data protection | Small limit sufficient for Year 1 |
| **Workers’ Compensation** | Required if any W-2 employees (including teaching artist if employee) | CA statutory |
| **Additional Insured Endorsements** | Property owners, city, sponsors, and teaching artists should be named as additional insured where required | Per contract |

## Part 4: Insurance Budget Reality Check

- Current budget assumption (Program page): $250/month ($3,000/year) for general liability + program insurance.
- This is reasonable for a small startup with no employees and one active panel at a time, but actual quotes may range from $2,000 to $6,000 annually depending on:
  - Whether minors participate in physical installation or only design
  - Whether the policy covers public right-of-way work
  - Whether teaching artists are contractors or employees
  - Whether abuse/molestation coverage is included
- **Recommendation:** Obtain three quotes and adjust budget if needed. Prioritize carriers with youth program experience.
