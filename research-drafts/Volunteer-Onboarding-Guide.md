# Sidewalk Stories LA: Volunteer Onboarding Guide

_Roles, expectations, safety protocols, and onboarding steps for adult volunteers supporting the Year 1 pilot. This guide should be sent to every new volunteer after they submit the website signup form._

---

## Welcome

Thank you for volunteering with Sidewalk Stories LA. Our work happens at the intersection of public safety, youth arts, and neighborhood pride. Whether you are photographing a panel, recruiting youth participants, or helping at an unveiling, you are part of making the city safer and more beautiful at the same time.

This guide covers what you can expect, what we expect from you, and how we keep everyone safe.

---

## Volunteer roles and time commitments

### 1. Photography and Documentation
- **What you do:** Capture before/after photos, document youth workshops and panel unveilings, create visual content for social media and grant reports.
- **Time commitment:** 2 to 4 hours per panel event; roughly 6 to 10 hours per month during active pilot.
- **Skills needed:** Own camera or smartphone; basic photo composition; respect for youth privacy and consent.

### 2. Community Outreach
- **What you do:** Distribute flyers, talk with neighbors and property owners, recruit youth participants, promote voting periods, attend neighborhood events.
- **Time commitment:** 2 to 4 hours per week during outreach pushes.
- **Skills needed:** Comfortable talking with strangers; familiarity with Fairfax / Mid-City West; bilingual Spanish or Korean is a plus.

### 3. Event Support
- **What you do:** Help set up and run panel unveiling ceremonies, community vote events, youth art workshops, and volunteer appreciation gatherings.
- **Time commitment:** 3 to 6 hours per event; 1 to 2 events per month.
- **Skills needed:** Reliable, punctual, comfortable working around youth and crowds.

### 4. Administrative and Grant Writing
- **What you do:** Assist with data entry, email correspondence, grant research, grant application drafting, social media scheduling, and website updates.
- **Time commitment:** 2 to 4 hours per week; remote friendly.
- **Skills needed:** Writing, organization, spreadsheet comfort, attention to detail.

### 5. Social Media
- **What you do:** Help create posts, schedule content, respond to comments and DMs, track engagement metrics.
- **Time commitment:** 1 to 3 hours per week.
- **Skills needed:** Familiarity with Instagram and Facebook; basic graphic design tools helpful.

## Eligibility

- Volunteers must be 18 or older.
- Youth participants have a separate signup process and are not considered volunteers.
- Volunteers working directly with minors must pass a background check.
- Volunteers working on public sidewalks during installation must attend a safety orientation.
- Everyone must sign a liability waiver and photo release before participating.

## Onboarding checklist

### Before your first shift
- [ ] Submit the volunteer signup form on the website
- [ ] Attend a 30-minute virtual or in-person orientation
- [ ] Review this guide and the Youth Safety Policy
- [ ] Sign the adult volunteer liability waiver and photo release
- [ ] Provide emergency contact information
- [ ] Confirm your volunteer role and availability

### For roles working with minors
- [ ] Complete and pass a Live Scan / background check (Sidewalk Stories LA covers the cost or provides instructions)
- [ ] Review the two-adult rule: never be alone one-on-one with a minor
- [ ] Complete the 20-minute youth protection training module

### For roles at panel installation events
- [ ] Attend the site safety orientation
- [ ] Confirm closed-toe shoes, gloves, and safety glasses if requested
- [ ] Review weather and hydration protocols

## Safety training overview

### General site safety
- Stay behind barriers and designated volunteer zones during concrete work.
- Do not enter the active work area while the concrete crew is operating equipment.
- Wear closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, and a hat for outdoor events.
- Carry water and stay hydrated, especially in warm months.
- Report any injury, hazard, or unsafe behavior to the site lead immediately.

### Youth protection
- Follow the two-adult rule at all times when working with minors.
- Do not share personal contact information with youth participants.
- Do not transport youth in personal vehicles.
- Report any disclosure of abuse, neglect, or safety concern to the site lead immediately.
- Maintain appropriate physical boundaries and professional communication.

### Photography and privacy
- Photograph youth only when a parent/guardian has signed the photo release.
- Do not photograph minors in ways that identify home addresses, school uniforms, or full names without explicit consent.
- When in doubt, blur faces or do not post.

## Background check policy

Sidewalk Stories LA requires a background check for any volunteer role that involves direct interaction with youth participants. This includes event support at youth workshops, teaching artist assistants, panel unveiling coordinators, and outreach volunteers who recruit minors.

- **Who pays:** Sidewalk Stories LA covers or reimburses the cost.
- **Process:** Volunteers receive a Live Scan fingerprinting request or an online background check link.
- **Frequency:** One background check is required initially; re-checks may be required every two years.
- **Confidentiality:** Results are kept confidential and reviewed only by the designated board member or HR lead.
- **Disqualification:** A criminal history involving violence, sexual offenses, or child endangerment will disqualify a volunteer from roles involving minors. Other offenses are reviewed case by case.

## Liability waiver reference

All volunteers must sign the Adult Volunteer Liability Waiver and Release before participating in any Sidewalk Stories LA activity. The waiver covers:
- Assumption of risk for outdoor work, photography, and event support
- Release of liability for Sidewalk Stories LA, its directors, officers, volunteers, contractors, and teaching artists
- Photo and video release allowing the organization to use volunteer images for promotional purposes
- Consent to emergency medical treatment if the volunteer cannot be reached

A parent or guardian signature is not required because volunteers are adults. The signed waiver is kept on file for the duration of volunteer service plus three years.

## Code of conduct

- Be respectful to youth, families, property owners, sponsors, city staff, and fellow volunteers.
- Represent Sidewalk Stories LA positively in person and online.
- Follow instructions from the site lead, teaching artist, and concrete crew.
- Do not use drugs or alcohol before or during volunteer activities.
- Do not engage in political campaigning, religious solicitation, or commercial promotion while wearing the Sidewalk Stories LA name.

---

## Volunteer appreciation plan

Sidewalk Stories LA tracks volunteer hours and recognizes contribution in the following ways:

- **Monthly shout-out:** One volunteer featured on Instagram and in the email newsletter.
- **Quarterly gathering:** Casual meetup for volunteers, teaching artists, and sponsors.
- **Annual impact report:** All logged volunteers named in the Year 1 impact report.
- **Panel credit:** Volunteers who support a specific panel are listed on the project story page.
- **Swag:** T-shirt or tote bag after 20 hours of service (pending budget).

## How to report a concern

If you see or experience anything that concerns you, contact:
- **Site lead at events:** Trevor Damyan or designated board member
- **Email:** trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org
- **Urgent safety or youth protection issue:** Call 911 if immediate danger exists, then notify Trevor.
