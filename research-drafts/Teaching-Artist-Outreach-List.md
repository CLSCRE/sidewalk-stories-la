## Questions to Ask Every Prospective Teaching Artist

1. Do you have experience designing and installing mosaics in outdoor/public settings?
2. Have you worked with youth ages 10 to 17 in hands-on art workshops?
3. Are you comfortable managing a group of 6 to 10 kids on a public sidewalk for two weekends?
4. Do you have any familiarity with City of LA sidewalk / public right-of-way permits, or willingness to learn with us?
5. What is your stipend expectation for a two-weekend panel project (design + installation guidance)?
6. Can you provide references from prior youth or community public art projects?
7. Do you have a portfolio of community-built mosaic work we can share with sponsors and funders?
8. Are you available for the first panel in Fall 2026 and up to 10 panels over the next year?
