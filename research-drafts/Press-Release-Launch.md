## Sidewalk Stories LA Launches Pilot Program to Turn Broken LA Sidewalks into Community Mosaics

**LOS ANGELES, CA, [DATE].** Sidewalk Stories LA, a new California nonprofit public benefit corporation, today announced the launch of its Year 1 pilot program in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood. The organization repairs damaged sidewalk panels and transforms them into community-built mosaic art designed and installed by neighborhood youth working alongside professional teaching artists and licensed concrete crews.

The pilot will complete 10 panels over 10 months, each one replacing a cracked, hazardous sidewalk panel with a vibrant mosaic. The total pilot budget is $38,700, or $2,700 per panel, covering concrete repair, mosaic materials, a teaching artist stipend, and the required City of Los Angeles permit.

### A four-step model

Sidewalk Stories LA operates through a simple, repeatable process:

1. **Snap a Photo.** Residents use a mobile app to photograph damaged sidewalk panels, automatically capturing the location.
2. **Community Votes.** The neighborhood votes on which panel matters most. The top-voted panel wins each month.
3. **Repair & Create.** A licensed concrete crew fixes the panel, and neighborhood kids work with a teaching artist to design and install a mosaic.
4. **Celebrate & Share.** The organization documents the before and after, celebrates the young artists, and moves to the next panel.

### Local impact

The Fairfax / Mid-City West pilot is intentionally small and visible. Each repaired panel removes a tripping hazard for seniors, parents with strollers, and people with mobility challenges. Each mosaic gives 6 to 10 neighborhood kids hands-on public art experience and a visible contribution to their neighborhood.

### Sponsorship and support

Local businesses and individuals can support the pilot in several ways:

- **$25** buys tile for one square foot.
- **$100** covers grout and sealant for a full panel.
- **$400** pays a teaching artist for one weekend.
- **$750** sponsors an entire panel and includes the sponsor’s logo on the mosaic plaque, professional photography, and social media recognition.
- **$2,700** funds one complete panel from cracked concrete to finished mosaic.

Sidewalk Stories LA is actively recruiting panel sponsors in the Fairfax, Melrose, and La Brea corridor.

### How to get involved

Residents can report cracked panels, donate, sponsor a panel, volunteer, or sign up youth participants through the organization’s website at [sidewalkstoriesla.org](https://sidewalkstoriesla.org).

## About Sidewalk Stories LA

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation that repairs broken sidewalk panels in Los Angeles and transforms them into community-built mosaic art. Founded in 2026 by Trevor Damyan, the organization brings together licensed concrete crews, professional teaching artists, and neighborhood youth to make sidewalks safer, more beautiful, and more community owned. The Year 1 pilot will complete 10 panels in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood. 501(c)(3) status is pending.
