## Post 8: FAQ

**Day:** Week 4, Wednesday
**Theme:** Common question
**Image suggestion:** Graphic with a single question in large text: "Do you need a permit to mosaic a sidewalk?" Answer in smaller text below. Use brand colors.

**Caption:**
FAQ: Do you need a permit to mosaic a sidewalk?

Yes. Any work in the public right of way requires a permit from the City of Los Angeles Bureau of Engineering or Bureau of Street Services. We also coordinate with the Department of Cultural Affairs for public art review.

Our team handles all permitting before any work begins. It is included in the $400 per panel city permit cost.

Safety and compliance come first. Then the art.

Have another question? Drop it in the comments or visit the FAQ page linked in bio.

## Post 9: Behind the Scenes

**Day:** Week 5, Tuesday
**Theme:** The messy middle
**Image suggestion:** Photo of tiles, grout, tools, or materials laid out flat. Use images/mosaic-tiles-colorful.jpg or images/mosaic-tiles.jpg. Caption-style text: "Every mosaic starts with a pile of broken tile."

**Caption:**
Behind the scenes: every mosaic starts with broken tile, grout, sealant, safety gear, and a lot of planning.

Before the first kid places a single tile, we:
- Photograph and assess the panel
- Confirm property owner consent
- Submit city permits
- Hire a licensed concrete crew
- Recruit the teaching artist and youth participants
- Source weather-resistant materials

The finished photo is the visible part. The permit, the contractor, the insurance, and the schedule are what make it real.
