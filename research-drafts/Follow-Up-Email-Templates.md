# Sidewalk Stories LA: Follow-Up Email Templates

_Four reusable HTML email templates in Trevor’s voice: city official follow-up, funder follow-up, sponsor follow-up, and volunteer follow-up. All body text uses Calibri 11pt, line-height 1.4, color #333333. No em dashes or en dashes. Replace bracketed placeholders before sending._

---

## Template 1: City official follow-up

**Subject:** Following up: sidewalk repair and mosaic pilot in Mid-City West

**Use when:** You have not received a response to an initial city inquiry within 5 to 7 business days.

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<body style="font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; line-height: 1.4; color: #333333;">
<p>Hi [Name],</p>

<p>I am Trevor Damyan, founder of Sidewalk Stories LA, a nonprofit startup that repairs damaged sidewalk panels and transforms them into community built mosaics designed by neighborhood youth and teaching artists.</p>

<p>I emailed your office on [date] with questions about [permit pathway / Safe Sidewalks LA rebate / public art review] for our pilot project in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood. I wanted to follow up and make sure my message reached the right person.</p>

<p>Our first panel is scheduled for this fall, so I am eager to confirm the correct permit and insurance requirements before we finalize fundraising and scheduling. I am happy to provide any additional details, meet for a brief call, or stop by your office if that would be helpful.</p>

  <li>Does the mosaic component require review by the Department of Cultural Affairs?</li>
  <li>What insurance and bonding are required for youth participants and public right of way work?</li>
</ul>

<p>Thank you for your time and for the work your office does to keep Los Angeles walkable. I look forward to hearing from you.</p>
```

## How to use these templates in Outlook

1. Create a new email in Outlook.
2. Click the three dots in the message ribbon, then click **HTML text** (or use the Insert > Text from File method depending on Outlook version).
3. Paste the HTML snippet above into the HTML body.
4. Replace all bracketed placeholders with real names, dates, and details.
5. Attach the relevant document as a PDF (One-Page-Funder-Prospectus.pdf for funders, Sponsor-Agreement-Template.pdf for sponsors, Volunteer-Onboarding-Guide.pdf for volunteers).
6. Save as a draft. Do not click Send until Trevor explicitly approves.
