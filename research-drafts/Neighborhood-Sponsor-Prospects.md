## Pitch talking points

1. **The problem is visible.** Cracked sidewalks are a daily hazard for your customers.
2. **The solution is visible too.** A sponsored mosaic panel puts your name on a permanent public art piece.
3. **It is locally targeted.** We are starting in Fairfax / Mid-City West, so your sponsorship improves the exact streets your customers walk.
4. **The content is included.** We provide professional photos, social media tags, and press release mentions.
5. **The cost is modest.** $750 is less than many single ad campaigns and produces a lasting asset.
