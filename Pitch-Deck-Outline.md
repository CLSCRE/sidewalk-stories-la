## Slide 2 — The Problem

**LA sidewalks are cracked, dangerous, and ignored.**

- Seniors trip. Wheelchairs get stuck. Strollers detour into the street.
- Responsibility is stuck between property owners and the city.
- Damaged panels sit for years because there is no clear path to fix them.

**Visual:** Three to four photos of badly cracked, lifted, or root damaged sidewalks in LA neighborhoods. Keep it raw and real.

---

## Slide 3 — The Idea

**What if fixing a sidewalk could also create public art?**

- Residents photograph damaged sidewalk panels through an app.
- The community votes on which one matters most.
- A licensed crew repairs the panel.
- Neighborhood kids and a teaching artist turn it into a mosaic.
- The result: safer streets, proud kids, and a visible story the whole neighborhood owns.

**Visual:** Simple process diagram: Photo Submit → Vote → Repair → Mosaic → Celebrate. Icons or photos for each step.

## Slide 4 — The Program Model

**Simple, repeatable, and built for LA.**

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Report | Resident snaps a photo via app | Community |
| 2. Vote | Top voted panel wins each month | Community |
| 3. Repair | Licensed concrete crew fixes the panel | Contractor |
| 4. Create | Teaching artist + kids design and build mosaic | Youth + Artist |
| 5. Share | Before/after content celebrates the story | Everyone |

**Visual:** Timeline or flowchart. Include a small map pin graphic for geolocation.

## Slide 5 — Who Benefits

**Three groups win every time.**

1. **Pedestrians and residents:** Safer sidewalks, especially seniors and people with mobility challenges.
2. **Youth participants:** Hands on public art experience, civic pride, and a visible contribution to their neighborhood.
3. **Local businesses and sponsors:** Permanent public recognition on a mosaic panel, social content, and neighborhood goodwill.

## Design Notes

- **Color palette:** Earth tones (concrete gray) + bright mosaic colors (turquoise, amber, terracotta). Not corporate blue.
- **Fonts:** One clean sans serif for headers, one readable serif or sans for body text.
- **Photos:** Real LA sidewalks, real kids, real mosaics. Stock photos are okay for placeholders but replace with actual pilot content as soon as possible.
- **Length:** Target 10 to 12 minutes of presentation time. No more than 90 seconds per slide.
- **Backup slides:** Keep 3 to 4 backup slides for detailed budget, app screenshot, and board bios in case questions arise.
