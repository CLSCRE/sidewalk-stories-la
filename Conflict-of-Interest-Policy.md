# Sidewalk Stories LA — Conflict of Interest Policy

_Adopted by the Board of Directors on [Date]_

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this Conflict of Interest Policy is to protect the interests of Sidewalk Stories LA (the "Corporation") when it is contemplating a transaction or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of an officer or director of the Corporation. This policy is intended to supplement, but not replace, any applicable state and federal laws governing conflicts of interest.

---

## Definitions

**Interested Person:** Any director, officer, or member of a committee with board-delegated powers who has a direct or indirect financial interest, as defined below, is an Interested Person.

**Financial Interest:** A person has a financial interest if the person has, directly or indirectly, through business, investment, or family:
1. An ownership or investment interest in any entity with which the Corporation has a transaction or arrangement, or
2. A compensation arrangement with the Corporation or with any entity or individual with which the Corporation has a transaction or arrangement, or
3. A potential ownership or investment interest in, or compensation arrangement with, any entity or individual with which the Corporation is negotiating a transaction or arrangement.

---

## Duty to Disclose

Each director, officer, and committee member shall disclose to the Board any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any financial interest, in any matter before the Board or a committee.

Disclosures shall be made in writing and filed with the Secretary of the Corporation. A copy of all disclosures shall be maintained in the corporate records.

---

## Procedures for Addressing Conflicts of Interest

1. **Presentation of Information.** After disclosure of the financial interest and all material facts, and after any discussion with the Interested Person, the Interested Person shall leave the Board or committee meeting while the matter is discussed and voted upon. The remaining Board or committee members shall decide whether the Corporation can obtain a more advantageous arrangement with reasonable effort.

2. **Recusal.** The Interested Person shall not vote on, or participate in the deliberation of, any matter in which he or she has a financial interest.

3. **Documentation.** The minutes of the Board or committee meeting shall reflect:
   - That the disclosure was made
   - That the Interested Person was absent during discussion and voting
   - The Board's or committee's decision regarding the matter

4. **Compensation.** No loans shall be made by the Corporation to any director, officer, or committee member. No compensation shall be paid to any Interested Person unless approved in advance by a vote of the disinterested directors.

---

## Periodic Review

The Board shall review this Conflict of Interest Policy annually and require each director, officer, and committee member to complete a Disclosure Statement at least once per year.
