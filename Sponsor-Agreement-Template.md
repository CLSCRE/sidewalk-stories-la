## 1. Purpose

Sidewalk Stories LA repairs broken sidewalk panels in Los Angeles neighborhoods and transforms them into community-built mosaic art designed and installed by local youth working with professional teaching artists. This agreement confirms the Sponsor's commitment to fund one (or more) mosaic panel(s) and the recognition Sidewalk Stories LA will provide in return.

## 4. Sponsor Recognition

In recognition of the Sponsor's support, Sidewalk Stories LA agrees to provide the following:

**For Each Sponsored Panel:**
- [ ] Sponsor business name prominently displayed on a permanent plaque or tile embedded in the completed mosaic panel.
- [ ] Professional before-and-after photography of the sponsored panel, delivered digitally within 30 days of completion.
- [ ] Social media campaign tagging the Sponsor's business account across Sidewalk Stories LA channels (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn).
- [ ] Press release mention naming the Sponsor as a supporting partner.
- [ ] Inclusion in the Sidewalk Stories LA annual impact report.
- [ ] Listing on the Sponsor page of sidewalkstoriesla.org with business name, logo, and link.

**Additional Benefits (if applicable):**
- [ ] Invitation to the panel unveiling ceremony and community celebration.
- [ ] Opportunity for Sponsor representatives to participate in a photo opportunity with youth artists.

## 5. Logo and Branding

Sponsor shall provide a high-resolution logo file (PNG or vector) for use in digital and print materials. Sidewalk Stories LA agrees to use the logo in a manner consistent with the Sponsor's brand guidelines, if provided.

## 6. Limitations

- Sidewalk Stories LA reserves the right to approve all final mosaic designs and ensure they align with the organization's mission and community values.
- Sponsor does not have editorial control over mosaic artwork, youth participant selection, or program operations.
- Sidewalk Stories LA is not responsible for delays caused by weather, permitting, or city approvals.

## 7. Cancellation

If Sidewalk Stories LA is unable to complete the sponsored panel within 12 months of receiving payment due to circumstances beyond its control, the Sponsor may elect to:
- [ ] Transfer the sponsorship to a different panel location, or
- [ ] Receive a full refund of the sponsorship amount.

## 8. Publicity

Sidewalk Stories LA and the Sponsor agree to cooperate in joint publicity efforts. Either party may issue press releases or social media posts about the partnership, provided the other party is given 48 hours advance notice and an opportunity to review for factual accuracy.

## 9. Indemnification

Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from its own negligence or willful misconduct.

## 10. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

## 11. Entire Agreement

This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, or agreements.
