## 5. BYLAWS

Bylaws are the governance rules for the nonprofit. Unlike an Operating Agreement, they are not filed with the state, but they are required for 501(c)(3) status and banking.

**Key sections to include:**

1. **Name and principal office**
2. **Purposes** (must align with Articles of Incorporation)
3. **Membership** (most public benefit nonprofits have no voting members; all power rests with the board)
4. **Board of Directors**
   - Minimum 3 directors (California best practice for 501(c)(3))
   - Terms (typically 2 or 3 years, staggered)
   - Removal and resignation
   - No compensation for board service (or only with recusal and arm's length approval)
5. **Officers** (President, Secretary, Treasurer; one person can hold multiple offices except President and Secretary cannot be the same person in CA)
6. **Board meetings** (quorum, notice, remote participation)
7. **Committees** (optional: advisory board, fundraising committee, program committee)
8. **Conflict of Interest policy** (required by IRS for 501(c)(3))
9. **Indemnification**
10. **Amendment of bylaws** (typically requires board majority or supermajority)

**Who signs:** All directors at the first organizational meeting.

## 7. 501(C)(3) TAX EXEMPTION APPLICATION

**Determine which form to use:**

- **Form 1023-EZ:** Streamlined online application. Eligibility requirements:
  - Projected annual gross receipts ≤ $50,000 in Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3
  - Total assets ≤ $250,000
  - Must answer all questions correctly (no churches, no hospitals, no supporting organizations, etc.)
  - Fee: $275
  - Processing: typically 2 to 4 weeks

- **Form 1023:** Full application. Required if projected receipts exceed $50,000 or if you are not eligible for the EZ form.
  - Fee: $600
  - Processing: typically 3 to 6 months

**Given the financial model shows Year 1 revenue of $44,600 (under the $50K threshold), Sidewalk Stories LA likely qualifies for Form 1023-EZ if asset levels stay low.**

**What you need before filing:**
- Filed Articles of Incorporation
- EIN
- Bylaws (adopted)
- Conflict of Interest policy (adopted)
- Narrative description of activities (2 to 3 pages: what you do, who benefits, fundraising plan)
- Financial projections for Year 1, Year 2, Year 3
- Compensation arrangements (none in Year 1)

## 8. CALIFORNIA TAX AND REGULATORY OBLIGATIONS

| Obligation | What it is | Due / Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual franchise tax** | $800 minimum tax every year, even with zero revenue | Due by the 15th day of the 4th month after formation. If you file in August 2026, first payment is due December 15, 2026. |
| **Statement of Information (SI-100)** | Update directors and officers annually | $20, due every year |
| **FTB tax exemption** | California exempts certain nonprofits from state income tax | File Form 3500 with FTB after 501(c)(3) approval |
| **Charitable solicitation registration** | Required to solicit donations in California | Register with CA Attorney General, Registry of Charitable Trusts |
| **Insurance** | General liability + youth program coverage | Obtain quotes before first panel |
