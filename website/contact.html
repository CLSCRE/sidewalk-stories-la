Get Involved

Join the Movement

There are four ways to help: donate, sponsor a panel, volunteer, or spread the word. Every contribution gets us closer to the next mosaic.

Four Ways to Help

How You Can Participate

Donate

Any amount helps. $25 buys tile. $100 covers part of the teaching artist stipend. $2,700 funds an entire panel from cracked concrete to finished mosaic.

Sponsor a Panel

Businesses and organizations can sponsor a full panel for $750 or more. Your logo appears on the mosaic, in all photography, and across our social channels.

Volunteer

We need help with event coordination, social media, photography, grant writing, and neighborhood outreach. No experience required, just enthusiasm.

Spread the Word

Follow us on Instagram and Facebook. Share our posts. Tell your neighbors. Tag us when you spot a cracked panel that needs love.

Reach Out

Contact Us

Have a question, idea, or cracked panel to report? Send us a message. We read every one.
