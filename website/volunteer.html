Volunteer With Us

Sidewalk Stories LA runs on community energy. Whether you are a photographer, event organizer, outreach specialist, or just someone who wants to help, we have a role for you.

Photography & Documentation

Capture before/after photos, document youth workshops, and create visual content for social media and grant reports.

Community Outreach

Spread the word in the neighborhood, distribute flyers, talk to property owners, and help recruit youth participants.

Event Support

Help organize and run panel unveiling ceremonies, community vote events, and youth art workshops.

Administrative & Grant Writing

Assist with fundraising research, grant applications, data entry, email correspondence, and social media management.

Volunteer Requirements

  • Must be 18 or older (youth participants have a separate signup)
  • Background check required for roles working with minors
  • Commit to at least 4 hours per month during active pilot
  • Attend one orientation session before starting
  • Sign liability waiver and photo release
