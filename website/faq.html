About the Program

What is Sidewalk Stories LA?

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit that repairs broken sidewalk panels and transforms them into community-built mosaic art. Neighborhood kids work with professional teaching artists and licensed concrete crews to design and install each mosaic. It is part public safety fix, part youth arts program, and part civic engagement project.

How does the program work?

Residents use our mobile app to photograph damaged sidewalk panels. The community votes on which panel matters most. A licensed concrete crew repairs the winning panel, and neighborhood kids work with a teaching artist to design and build a mosaic on the fresh surface. We document the process and move to the next panel.

Where is the pilot happening?

Our Year 1 pilot is focused on the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood of Los Angeles, near Blackburn Avenue and Melrose. This is where our founder, Trevor Damyan, lives and walks every day. We selected this area because it has visible sidewalk damage and an active community that cares about neighborhood improvement.

How many panels will you complete in Year 1?

We plan to complete 10 panels in Year 1, at a rate of roughly one panel per month. Each panel takes about two weekends: one for design with the kids, and one for installation. The remaining months are for documentation, reporting, and planning Year 2 scaling.

Donations & Sponsorships

How much does one panel cost?

Each panel costs $2,700 total. That breaks down to $900 for concrete repair by a licensed crew, $1,000 for mosaic materials (tile, grout, sealant, safety gear), $400 for the teaching artist stipend, and $400 for the city right-of-way permit. Sponsors can fund a full panel for $750, with their logo on the mosaic plaque.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation. We are in the process of applying for 501(c)(3) federal tax-exempt status. Once approved, donations will be tax-deductible retroactively. In the meantime, we can accept donations and will provide receipts for your records. Please consult your tax advisor for specific guidance.

What do sponsors receive?

Panel sponsors ($750) receive: their business name on a permanent plaque embedded in the mosaic, professional before-and-after photography, social media campaign tagging their account, press release mention, and listing on our website. Neighborhood sponsors ($5,000) and Founding Partners ($10,000+) receive expanded recognition across all Year 1 materials.

Can I donate materials instead of money?

Yes, in-kind donations are welcome. We can accept weather-resistant tile, grout, sealant, safety gear for kids, and professional photography services. Please contact us at trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org to discuss in-kind contributions and receive a donation receipt for the fair market value of materials.

Volunteering & Participation

How do I sign up my child to participate?

Youth participants are recruited through local schools, community centers, and neighborhood networks in the pilot area. If you live in or near Fairfax / Mid-City West and have a child aged 10-17 interested in public art, email us at trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org. We will add you to the participant list and send details once the first panel is scheduled.

Can adults volunteer?

Yes, we need adult volunteers for community outreach, event support, photography, documentation, and administrative tasks. All volunteers working directly with youth must complete a background check. Sign up on our volunteer page or email trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org.

Do participants need art experience?

No prior art experience is required. Our teaching artist designs workshops for all skill levels. The process is collaborative: kids sketch patterns, choose colors, and learn mosaic techniques together. The teaching artist guides every step, from initial design to final grout and sealant.

Legal & Safety

Do you need a permit to mosaic a sidewalk?

Yes. Any work in the public right-of-way requires a permit from the City of Los Angeles Bureau of Street Services or Bureau of Engineering. We also coordinate with the Department of Cultural Affairs for public art review. Our team handles all permitting before any work begins. This is included in the $400 per panel city permit cost.

Is it safe for kids to work on public sidewalks?

Safety is our top priority. All youth participants wear safety gear (gloves, goggles, closed-toe shoes). The teaching artist leads all sessions and enforces safety protocols. A licensed concrete crew handles all structural work. We carry general liability and youth program insurance. Parents sign a liability waiver before participation.

Who owns the mosaic after it is installed?

The mosaic becomes part of the public sidewalk infrastructure. The artwork is maintained by the property owner adjacent to the panel, consistent with standard city sidewalk maintenance responsibilities. Sidewalk Stories LA retains the right to document and photograph all panels for promotional, educational, and grant reporting purposes.
