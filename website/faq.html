About the Program

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit that repairs broken sidewalk panels and transforms them into community-built mosaic art. Neighborhood kids work with professional teaching artists and licensed concrete crews to design and install each mosaic. It is part public safety fix, part youth

Residents use our mobile app to photograph damaged sidewalk panels. The community votes on which panel matters most. A licensed concrete crew repairs the winning panel, and neighborhood kids work with a teaching artist to design and build a mosaic on the fresh surface. We

Is it safe for kids to work on public sidewalks?

Safety is our top priority. All youth participants wear safety gear (gloves, goggles, closed-toe shoes). The teaching artist leads all sessions and enforces safety protocols. A licensed concrete crew handles all structural work. We carry general liability and youth program insurance. Parents sign a liability waiver before participation.

Who owns the mosaic after it is installed?

The mosaic becomes part of the public sidewalk infrastructure. The artwork is maintained by the property owner adjacent to the panel, consistent with standard city sidewalk maintenance responsibilities.
