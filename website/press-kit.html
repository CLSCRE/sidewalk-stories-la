Sidewalk Stories LA at a Glance

Founded: 2026 by Trevor Damyan in Los Angeles, CA

Mission: Repair broken sidewalks and transform them into community mosaics built by local youth

Pilot: 10 panels in Year 1 in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood

Cost per panel: $2,700 (concrete, tile, artist, permit)

Youth engaged: 6 to 10 kids per panel; 60 to 100 in Year 1

Sponsor cost: $750 per panel with logo on mosaic

Website: sidewalkstoriesla.org

Contact: trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org

Team

Founding Board Bios

Trevor Damyan, Founder & Board President

Trevor Damyan is the founder of Sidewalk Stories LA, an emerging Los Angeles nonprofit initiative developing a sidewalk-safety and youth public-art pilot in Fairfax and Mid-City West.

He lives in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood of Los Angeles and walks past cracked sidewalk panels every day. With a decade of experience in structured finance and nonprofit formation, Trevor brings disciplined project management and community relationship building to the civic art space.

Trevor is a member of The Pottery Room in Culver City and regularly attends Urban Land Institute meetings, where he tracks LA infrastructure and pedestrian safety trends.

Contact: trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org

Jason Ciment, Founding Tech Advisor

Jason Ciment is a co-founder of GetVisible and a digital marketing entrepreneur who advises Sidewalk Stories LA on technology and digital engagement.

His experience with a campaign sidewalk-reporting platform helped inform the nonprofit’s approach. Sidewalk Stories LA is developing and owning its current community reporting platform in-house.

Jason is loosely associated as a technology sounding board without day-to-day nonprofit operations or fiduciary duties.

Meredith Reed, Director & Board Secretary

Meredith Reed is a founding board member and Board Secretary of Sidewalk Stories LA. She is an attorney, operations executive, and public art advocate who has never encountered a crumbling public asset she didn’t want to save.

On the board, Meredith keeps governance tight—notices, minutes, and the corporate record book—and helps connect the nonprofit to Los Angeles teaching artists, cultural partners, and civic allies as the youth mosaic pilot gets underway.

Contact: meredith@sidewalkstoriesla.org

JD

Jim De Vico, Director & Operations Lead

Jim De Vico is a founding board member and Operations Lead of Sidewalk Stories LA. A Principal and Fractional COO at Praxis LA, he brings operating discipline and implementation experience to the organization.

Jim helps translate board priorities into practical systems for partner coordination, vendor management, and disciplined pilot execution.

Contact: jim@sidewalkstoriesla.org
