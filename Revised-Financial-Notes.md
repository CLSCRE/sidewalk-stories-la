# Sidewalk Stories LA — Financial Model Revision Notes

_The current model shows a Year 1 net loss of $120,900 on 48 panels. That is not credible for a startup nonprofit approaching funders. Here is how to fix it._

---

## The Problem

Under current assumptions:
- 48 panels Year 1 = $129,600 in variable costs
- Fixed costs = $32,400/year
- Startup costs = $3,500
- Revenue = $44,600 (individual donations, sponsors, two small grants, events)
- **Net loss: $120,900**

No grant officer will fund a first year program with a $121K hole unless you are sitting on a major foundation commitment. The current model assumes scale (48 panels) without the revenue to match.

---

## The Fix: Pilot Phase Model

**Reduce Year 1 to a credible pilot: 6 to 12 panels.**

This gives you:
- A manageable scope to prove the concept
- A smaller funding ask that matches realistic first year grant sizes
- Content and outcomes to show Year 2 funders
- Time to build community engagement and app usage before scaling

---

## Year 2 Scaling Model

Once the pilot produces 10 finished panels, community content, and a track record:

| Metric | Year 2 |
|---|---|
| Panels completed | 30 |
| Revenue (grants + sponsors + donations) | $95,000 |
| Total costs | $91,000 |
| **Net income** | **$4,000** |

Year 2 is break-even or slightly positive. Year 3 scales to 60+ panels with a stable revenue mix.

## What to Change in the Excel Model

1. **Add a "Pilot Scenario" tab** with the revised numbers above.
2. **Keep the original 48-panel tab** as a "Growth Scenario" for Year 2+ conversations.
3. **Add a grant detail tab:** list target funders, award sizes, application deadlines, and probability of success.
4. **Add a "Fiscal Sponsor Scenario" tab:** if Sidewalk Stories LA operates under an existing nonprofit in Year 1, overhead drops to near zero (no insurance, no bookkeeping, no entity costs). Only variable panel costs and artist stipends remain.
