## Section 2: Statement of Need (1 to 2 pages)

**The Problem:**

Thousands of miles of damaged sidewalks across Los Angeles create daily hazards for seniors, people with mobility challenges, and families with strollers. The City of Los Angeles estimates the repair backlog is measured in years, not months. Responsibility for repair is often unclear between property owners and the city, which means many damaged panels go unaddressed for years.

A single lifted sidewalk panel is more than an inconvenience. For a senior with a walker, it is a fall risk. For a person in a wheelchair, it is a barrier. For a parent with a stroller, it forces a detour into the street.

**The Data:**

- The City of LA estimates thousands of miles of damaged sidewalks across the city.
- A single panel repair can cost a homeowner $1,500 or more, which means many simply wait.
- The Safe Sidewalks LA rebate program exists but uptake is limited by awareness and process complexity.

**Why This Neighborhood:**

The Fairfax / Mid-City West area has visible sidewalk damage, active community engagement, and a property owner willing to partner on the first panel. It is a neighborhood where a small, visible project can build momentum quickly.

## Section 3: Project Description (2 to 3 pages)

**Overview:**

Sidewalk Stories LA operates on a simple, repeatable process:

1. **Report:** Residents photograph damaged sidewalk panels through our mobile app, automatically capturing geolocation.
2. **Vote:** The community votes on which damaged panels matter most. The top-voted panel wins each month.
3. **Repair:** A licensed concrete crew removes the damaged panel, preps the base, and pours new concrete.
4. **Create:** A teaching artist leads neighborhood kids in designing and building a mosaic on the fresh surface.
5. **Celebrate:** We document the before and after, celebrate the young artists, and move to the next panel.

**What Makes This Different:**

We do not just fix sidewalks. We turn the repair process into a public art and youth education moment. Every panel becomes a story the neighborhood tells together. Unlike traditional public art grants that fund a single installation, Sidewalk Stories LA creates a repeatable, neighborhood-based model with quick, visible outcomes.

**Youth Engagement:**

Each panel engages 6 to 10 neighborhood kids across two weekends. The first weekend is design: sketching patterns, choosing colors, learning mosaic techniques. The second weekend is installation: cutting tile, setting grout, sealing the surface. Kids gain skills in design, teamwork, and public collaboration. They also build a direct connection to civic life at an early age.

**Teaching Artist Role:**

Our teaching artist guides all youth sessions, manages materials, and ensures the finished mosaic is weather-resistant and durable. The artist is paid a $400 stipend per panel.
