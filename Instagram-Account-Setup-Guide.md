## Account Setup Instructions

1. **Download the Instagram app** on your phone or go to instagram.com on desktop.
2. **Sign up** with email: trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org (or create a new Gmail like sidewalkstoriesla@gmail.com if preferred).
3. **Username:** @sidewalkstoriesla
4. **Password:** Use a strong password and save it in your password manager.
5. **Profile Type:** Switch to a **Business Account** after setup (Settings > Account > Switch to Professional Account > Business).
6. **Category:** Nonprofit Organization / Community Organization.
7. **Contact:** Add email trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org and website sidewalkstoriesla.org.
8. **Profile Photo:** Use the Sidewalk Stories LA logo when available, or a colorful mosaic tile pattern as placeholder.
