## After Filing: Next Steps (in order)

1. **Get EIN from IRS** (free, online at irs.gov)
   - Entity type: "Other nonprofit organization"
   - Use the filed Articles of Incorporation as supporting document

2. **Draft Bylaws** (template to be created)
   - Must have at least 3 directors total (can start with 1 and add later)
   - Define officer roles: President, Secretary, Treasurer
   - Set meeting frequency and quorum rules

3. **Hold Organizational Meeting**
   - Elect officers
   - Adopt bylaws
   - Authorize bank account opening
   - Minute the meeting

4. **Open Nonprofit Bank Account**
   - Bring: filed Articles, EIN letter, bylaws, board resolution, ID
   - Recommended: local credit union or a bank with nonprofit expertise (e.g., Amalgamated Bank, Beneficial State Bank, or a local LA credit union)

5. **Apply for 501(c)(3) Status**
   - Form 1023-EZ if eligible (gross receipts under $50,000/year, assets under $250,000): $275 fee
   - Full Form 1023 if above thresholds: $600 fee
   - Processing time: 2-6 months for EZ; 6-12 months for full Form 1023

6. **Apply for CA Tax Exemption**
   - File Form 3500 with CA Franchise Tax Board (FTB)
   - Or file Form 3500A if you have IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter

7. **Register with CA Attorney General (Registry of Charitable Trusts)**
   - Required if soliciting donations in CA
   - File Form CT-1 within 30 days of receiving assets
