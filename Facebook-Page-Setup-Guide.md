## About Section (Copy and Paste)

**Short Version (255 characters max for preview):**

Sidewalk Stories LA repairs broken sidewalks and transforms them into community mosaics built by neighborhood youth and professional teaching artists. Pilot launching Fall 2026 in Fairfax / Mid-City West.

**Long Version (in the About tab):**

Sidewalk Stories LA is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation with a simple mission: improve public safety and youth civic engagement by repairing damaged sidewalks and transforming them into community-built mosaic art.

Here is how it works:
1. Residents use our mobile app to photograph damaged sidewalk panels
2. The community votes on which panel matters most
3. A licensed concrete crew repairs the winning panel
4. Neighborhood kids work with a professional teaching artist to design and install a mosaic on the fresh surface
5. We document, celebrate, and move to the next panel

Our Year 1 pilot will complete 10 panels in the Fairfax / Mid-City West neighborhood of Los Angeles, engaging roughly 60 to 100 local youth in public art creation.

Each panel costs $2,700 total: $900 for concrete repair, $1,000 for mosaic materials, $400 for the teaching artist stipend, and $400 for the city right-of-way permit.

We are currently seeking grant partners, corporate sponsors, and community supporters for our first 10 panels. Every dollar funds concrete, tile, or a stipend for a teaching artist.

Learn more: sidewalkstoriesla.org
Contact: trevor@sidewalkstoriesla.org
