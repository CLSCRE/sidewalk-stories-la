---

## Content Pillars (What to Post)

### 1. Before/After Transformation
The core visual story. Show the cracked panel next to the finished mosaic.
- **Before launch:** Use stock photos or AI-generated split-screen concepts.
- **After launch:** Real before/after photography from every panel.

### 2. Youth Artists in Action
Kids designing, sketching, cutting tile, setting grout. Faces visible with parental consent.
- **Before launch:** Stock or illustrative content with captions about what kids will do.
- **After launch:** Real photos of youth participants.

### 3. Teaching Artist Spotlights
Feature the teaching artist's background, process, and philosophy.
- **Before launch:** Introduce Patricia (or whoever is selected) with a headshot and bio.
- **After launch:** Process videos and artist commentary.

### 4. Neighborhood Stories
Why this panel matters. Interview the resident who submitted it, the property owner, or a neighbor who walks by it every day.
- **Before launch:** Trevor walking the neighborhood, pointing out cracked panels.
- **After launch:** Resident testimonials and community reactions.

### 5. Sponsorship Recognition
Tag and thank sponsors. Show their logo on the panel plaque.
- **Before launch:** Announce sponsor partnerships.
- **After launch:** Sponsor spotlights with panel photos.

### 6. Behind the Scenes
Permits, concrete pours, tile selection, city approvals. The messy middle that makes the final product meaningful.
- **Before launch:** City outreach updates, material sourcing.
- **After launch:** Real process documentation.

### 7. Educational Content
How mosaics are made, the history of public art in LA, why sidewalk repair matters for seniors and accessibility.
- **Always relevant.** Mix of carousel posts, single images, and Reels.

---

## Posting Frequency

**Phase 1 (Pre-launch):** 2-3 posts per week  
**Phase 2 (Pilot active):** 4-5 posts per week  
**Phase 3 (Scaling):** 5-7 posts per week
