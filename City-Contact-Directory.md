# Sidewalk Stories LA — City Contact Directory

_Research completed 2026-07-20. All contacts verified via official city websites. Use email first, phone as backup._

---

## 1. Safe Sidewalks LA Rebate Program (Bureau of Engineering)

**Program:** Reimburses property owners up to $12,400 per lot for ADA-compliant sidewalk repair.
**Status:** Currently on waitlist due to funding constraints. New applications placed in queue.
**Website:** [sidewalks.lacity.gov/rebates](https://sidewalks.lacity.gov/rebates)
**Email:** sidewalks@lacity.org
**Phone:** 311 (MyLA311)
**Online:** [MyLA311 portal](https://myla311.lacity.org/)
**Mobile:** MyLA311 app (Apple/Android)

**Key question for Sidewalk Stories LA:** Can a nonprofit partner with a homeowner to manage the repair and mosaic work while the homeowner captures the rebate? Or can the nonprofit itself apply if it obtains permission from the adjacent property owner?

## 2. Bureau of Engineering — Sidewalk Repair Access Request Program

**Program:** Prioritizes sidewalk repair for people with mobility disabilities.
**Wait time:** 3 to 6 years for high-priority; 10+ years for others.
**Website:** [engineering.lacity.gov/about-us/divisions/sidewalk-repair-program](https://engineering.lacity.gov/about-us/divisions/sidewalk-repair-program)
**Email:** sidewalks@lacity.org
**Access Request Online:** [MyLA311 Access Program](https://myla311.lacity.org/AccessProgram)
**Mail:** Department on Disability, 201 N Figueroa Street, Suite 100, Los Angeles, CA 90012

**Media contact:** Mary Nemick, Director of Communications
**Email:** Mary.Nemick@lacity.org

**Street Tree/Sidewalk Projects:** Hector Banuelos, Street Tree Superintendent I
**Email:** Hector.banuelos@lacity.org

## 3. Bureau of Street Services (StreetsLA)

**Role:** Primarily handles tree-related work and permits. For actual sidewalk repair, BOE handles it.
**Website:** [streets.lacity.gov](https://streets.lacity.gov)
**General Email:** StreetsLA.ERT@lacity.org (External Relations Team)
**City Directory:** bss.boss@lacity.org
**Service Requests:** 311@lacity.org

**Note:** If your inquiry involves tree root damage lifting a sidewalk panel, StreetsLA is the right contact. If it is a general crack or structural failure, BOE handles it.

## Recommended Outreach Order

1. **sidewalks@lacity.org** (Safe Sidewalks LA / BOE) — Ask about nonprofit participation in rebate program and right-of-way permit requirements.
2. **dca.review@lacity.org** (DCA) — Ask about public art review process for sidewalk mosaics.
3. **outreach@midcitywest.org** (Neighborhood Council) — Introduce the project and ask about community improvement grant process.
4. **StreetsLA.ERT@lacity.org** (StreetsLA) — Only if tree-related damage is involved.
